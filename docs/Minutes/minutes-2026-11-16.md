## 4. Business model

The professor gave three ways for us to make money from the chain. They can be combined, and each one calls for different data in the reports.

**Decision:** we combine options 1 and 2. The chain pays a monthly plan whose price depends on the number of scooters and the included km; each km above the included ones is charged separately. Couriers as a service are left out.

| Option | How it works | What the system has to record | Adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Plan with an included limit | The chain pays a fixed amount per month with a usage limit (for example, X km per store); above the limit it pays separately | Accumulated usage per store and per month, and the excess over the limit | Yes |
| 2. Charge per use | The chain pays per km travelled, or per time of use | Km and duration of each use, from the telemetry | Yes |
| 3. Couriers as a service | HiperEats also supplies couriers, besides the scooters | HiperEats couriers, their hours or deliveries, and the store they are assigned to | No |

In all the options, the system produces usage reports that serve as the basis for charging. Issuing the invoices to the chain is out of scope, because the assignment excludes invoicing between companies.

## 5. Business rule values

| Rule | Decided value |
|---|---|
| Minimum battery to reserve | Calculated from the delivery distance (round trip plus a 20% margin), checked for each delivery |
| Low battery during use | Alert at 15% |
| Peak hours | 11:30 to 14:30 and 19:00 to 22:00, every day |
| Maintenance SLA | 2 h at peak, 8 h off-peak |
| Time that counts for the SLA | The deadline gets shorter when the peak starts. Ticket opened at peak: opening plus 2 h. Opened off-peak: the earlier of opening plus 8 h and start of the next peak plus 2 h |
| Maximum duration of a use | 4 h (one shift) |
| Return tolerance | 15 min |
| Overtime | €2 per started 15 min |
| Deposit | Contract clause that charges the chain the value of the damaged or missing scooter (to confirm with the professor, section 9) |
| Validity of a reservation without pick-up | 15 min |
| Simultaneous uses per courier | 1 |
| Km included in the plan | 300 km per scooter per month |
| Price per km above the plan | €0.10 |
| Rules that the chain manager configures | Reservation validity and low-battery alert during use; the others are fixed |

## 6. Missing values (HE-15)

Section 3.2 of the Product Specification already fixes the radius (BR2, 3 km), the battery margin (BR1, ×1.2), the included km (BR15) and the deposit (BR11). Still missing are the range of each scooter type, which BR1 uses without defining it, and the values of the order generator and of the simulator.

André brought a proposal, but the values **have not been decided yet**; they are to be closed by Tuesday, 17 Nov (section 8).

| Value | Proposal | Why, and boundary values |
|---|---|---|
| Full range, standard scooter | 25 km (uses 4% of battery per km) | BR1, delivery at 2 km: needs 4.8 km; with 20% battery it has 5.0 km and accepts, with 19% it has 4.75 km and refuses |
| Full range, long range scooter | 50 km (uses 2% of battery per km) | BR1, delivery at 3 km: needs 7.2 km; with 15% it has 7.5 km and accepts, with 14% it has 7.0 km and refuses |
| Delivery radius | 3 km, as already in BR2 | Only to confirm; nothing changes in the spec |
| Generator: hours | Generates orders only while the store is open, from 10:00 to 23:00 | 09:59 does not generate, 10:00 generates |
| Generator: rate per store | 1 order every 3 min at peak hours, 1 every 15 min off-peak | Uses the peak hours of BR9. With 10 scooters per store (BR13), at peak the fleet is almost fully busy, which exercises the "no scooter available" case |
| Generator: addresses | 60% within 2 km, 35% between 2 and 3 km, 5% outside the radius | Exercises BR14 (standard or long range) and the refusal of BR2. In the tests we use a fixed seed, so that the results are reproducible |
| Simulator: stores and fleet | 3 stores with 10 scooters each (the capacity of BR13): 7 standard and 3 long range | The proportion follows the addresses: about a third of the deliveries need long range |
| Simulator: speed | 20 km/h on average (the legal limit is 25 km/h) | A delivery at 3 km takes 18 min, there and back |
| Simulator: telemetry | One message per scooter every 5 s (battery, position, lock) | With 30 scooters that is 6 messages per second, below the 10 per second of SLO2 |
| Simulator: faults | 2% of the uses generate a fault | Feeds the maintenance flow and the SLA (BR9) without flooding the system with tickets |

The values of the generator and of the simulator are configurable through environment variables, so that the load tests with k6 can raise the rate. Once decided, they go into the Product Specification: the ranges in BR1, and those of the generator and of the simulator in a table in section 4.

The extensions (geofencing and predictive maintenance) stay out until we decide to go ahead with them.

### Story format

>
> **Acceptance criteria:**
> - Only scooters from the courier's own store are shown.
> - Only scooters in the available state are shown.
