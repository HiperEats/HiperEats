## 4. Approved concretization

A fictional hypermarket chain with restaurants hires HiperEats, as the operator, to manage and maintain a fleet of electric scooters at its stores. Maintenance is the responsibility of HiperEats, within the agreed SLA. Couriers pick up a scooter at the store, deliver the meals to nearby customers and return it to the hub. The product is fleet management; the food orders are only simulated.

### Actors

| Actor in the assignment | In HiperEats |
|---|---|
| End user | Courier (hypermarket employee; there are no external couriers) |
| Operator and technician | Company hired to manage the fleet: registers scooters and hubs, does maintenance within the SLA, swaps batteries |
| Institutional client | Manager of the hypermarket chain: sets the rules of use and follows the KPIs and SLA compliance |
| Admin | Manages accounts and roles |
| External systems | Telemetry simulator, payment sandbox, simulated order generator |

### Candidate business rules

- **Minimum battery:** a scooter is assigned only if it has enough battery for the round trip, with a margin.
- **Delivery radius:** the scooter cannot leave the store's delivery area.
- **Shifts:** the courier picks up a scooter only during their shift and has to return it at the end, or face a penalty.
- **Fixed fleet per store:** each hypermarket has a fixed number of scooters, which do not move to other stores. The scooter is returned to the store it left from.
- **Maintenance SLA:** shorter at peak hours.

## 5. Questions raised

**Decided:**

- The customers who receive the food do not appear in the system. Orders are created by a simulated generator, with only a delivery address and a time.
- There are several stores, each with its own fixed fleet. The rules of use are the same for all of them, and the chain manager compares the KPIs across stores.

**To ask the professor:**

- **Payments:** the MVP requires charging a deposit and overtime costs in a sandbox. If all couriers are employees, who pays and why? For example, the courier pays a penalty for returning the scooter after the end of the shift or with damage caused by negligence.
- **Fleet owner:** do the scooters belong to the hypermarket chain or to HiperEats? In both cases, maintenance is done by HiperEats.

**Open:**

- Which extensions do we want to try? Candidates: geofencing by delivery radius, predictive maintenance and notifications of SLA at risk.
